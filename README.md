## About
The four of us decided to collaborate on this project for our first year CI10X sequence during our time as CS majors at Drexel SCIS (formerly called CCI).
It is an action adventure RPG inspired by the Zelda and Pokemon series. Our pitch was the following:
"The open-world Zelda formula has been the same for nearly a decade. While Breath of the Wild was groundbreaking upon its 2017 release, recent games have
drained its formula of all of its juice, leaving players thirsty for something fresh.

Our untitled free-to-play game draws inspiration from older Zelda entries as players explore an open world environment from a top-down view.
Players can battle various fantasy enemies, interact with the environment in puzzling ways, and explore unique dungeons themed around regional biomes.
By blending the classic Zelda formula with concepts from other modern RPGs, our game will throw a unique spin on the action-adventure genre."

Our sights were quite ambitious, and we were only able to get the tutorial region complete by the end of the 15-week span of the project. Despite this,
we are still extremely proud of what we built in the end.
